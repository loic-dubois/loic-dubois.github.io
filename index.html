Postdoctoral researcher at University of Notre Dame (USA) since October 2025, supervised by Erin Chambers. My research interests are in graph algorithms, computational and combinatorial geometry and topology, and topological data analysis. I also have general interests in geometry processing, combinatorial optimization, and AI.

From September 2022 to September 2025, I was a PhD student at Université Gustave Eiffel (France), supervised by Éric Colin de Verdière and Vincent Despré. Before that, I studied at École Normale Supérieure de Lyon (France).
